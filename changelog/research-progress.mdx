    # **Changelog - Jan 27 2025**

    ## **Slack Integration**

    Connect Slack to Momo!

    ### **How to Connect**

    1. Go to **Settings** → **Connected Apps**
    2. Click **Connect Slack**
    3. Select the channels you want to monitor

    ### **What Gets Extracted as Decisions**

    Messages you've sent, replied to in threads, you're mentioned in are extracted. To see other people's slack messages, you need to create a team and invite them.

    ## **Daily Brief & Chat Tools**

    Couple of new features in chat tab.

    ### **Available Tool Calls by Integration**

    1. **Gmail:** Read, search, send, and reply to emails directly. 2) \*\*Github: \*\*Browse your repositories, commits, PRs, what changed. 3) \*\*Notion: \*\*Find pages and databases, read and update content, and create new pages or database entries. 4) **Slack:** See your channels and messages, search conversations, check mentions, and send or reply to messages. (when you message from Momo, it sends as Momo bot app in slack) 5) **Linear:** View teams and projects, track issues, search tasks, and create or inspect work tickets.

    ## **User Profile Update**

    Update your profile picture in Settings \> About me.

    ## **Teams**

    You can only see other teammates' work when you 1) create a team, 2) invite them, 3) and when they connect to apps.

    # **Changelog - Jan 28 2025**

    ## **Slackbot**

    You can now talk to Momo on slack.

    - To chat with it, you need to integrate Slack app in settings.
    - The daily query limit is 10 for both in the website and in Momo slackbot.
    - For those who already connected Slack, you might need to reinstall the Momo bot (just reconnect slack) to start chatting.

    # **Changelog - Jan 30 2025**

    ## **Discord**

    You can now connect with Discord! 

    - free plan: connect to 1 server, 3 channels
    - settings \> connected apps \> discord
    - you can only extract decisions based on messages you've sent & replied to & mentioned.
    - chat with Momo in the discord server with a slash command: `/momo question: hi momo`
    - chat queries limited to 10 a day (including messages sent from slack, website)
